Lilly Cherian has spent a career moving between the classroom and the page, teaching literary theory while writing essays that range from the personal to the critical — on ageing and identity as readily as on Jakobson, Derrida, and feminist literary criticism.

Her essays on this site fall into two loose families: assorted musings drawn from everyday life, and closer readings of the postmodern and post-structuralist theory she has taught for years. She writes, by her own account, because the question "who am I?" turned out to have more interesting partial answers than a single one.
