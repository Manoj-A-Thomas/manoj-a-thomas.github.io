Prof Lilly Cherian

Essayist & literary critic

Professor Lilly Cherian has spent a lifetime moving between the classroom, where she taught literary theory by day, and pages of literary books, where she spent whatever hours were left.

Her extraordinary double life shows in the essays collected here, which fall into two loose categories: assorted musings drawn from lived experience, and close readings of the postmodern and post-structuralist theory she spent years teaching.

Professor Cherian is the author of "Across the Vista of Years", written, by her own account, in the twilight zone of her life. The book is a record of her own years and the decades that carried her through them. It is not a story of famous people or famous events. It is, as she puts it, the story of ordinary people who led ordinary lives, the kind who live and die "unhonoured and unsung." She keeps writing, she says, to give those people an identity, so that the memories that would otherwise fade too soon might linger a little longer.
